# Data licences

The statistics in this map come from the sources listed on the site (About & sources) and in
`pipeline/data/sources_registry.csv`. The map geometry combines:

- **Natural Earth** 1:10m cultural and physical vectors — public domain.
- **geoBoundaries** (Runfola, D. et al. 2020, *geoBoundaries: A global database of political administrative boundaries*,
  PLOS ONE 15(4): e0231866, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231866) — per-file licences below.
- **UK local authorities** — Office for National Statistics, Open Government Licence v3.0. Contains OS data
  © Crown copyright and database right 2021.
- **India districts (Census 2011)** — DataMeet community-created maps of India.
- **Line of control in Ukraine** — DeepState map (17 October 2026).

Because several geoBoundaries files are derived from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0), the map
data files published here (`map*.json`) are made available under the **Open Database License (ODbL 1.0)**. Code in this
repository: MIT.
